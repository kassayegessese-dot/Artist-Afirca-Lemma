About Me

Africa Lema Head of Culture & Tourism

Veteran Konso Traditional Singer | Cultural Ambassador

I am Africa Lema, currently serving as the Head of Culture and Tourism Office of Konso Zone, Ethiopia. I have dedicated many years to preserving the unique cultural heritage of the Konso people while promoting sustainable tourism and empowering local communities.

Besides my public service, I am recognized as one of the veteran Konso traditional singers, using music to preserve our history, language, customs, and identity for future generations.

My mission is to connect culture, tourism, education, and community development to create lasting value for Konso and Ethiopia.
